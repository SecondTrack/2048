# 2048

**The number puzzle, native and offline – in the original colors.**

Swipe to slide all tiles; two equal tiles merge into one. Reach 2048. A new 2 (or, one time in ten, a 4) appears after every move.

## What it can do

- Swipe in four directions, small animation for new tiles.
- Your best score is saved.
- Works completely offline, no ads, no tracking.

## Runs on

iOS 6.1.6 · jailbroken iPod touch 4

## Install

Easiest via the **[Reboot App Store](https://github.com/SecondTrack/reboot)** right on the iPod. By hand: `.deb` from the [latest release](https://github.com/SecondTrack/2048/releases/latest), `dpkg -i`, then `uicache`.

Build it yourself with [Theos](https://theos.dev): `make package` (iOS 6.0+, armv7).
